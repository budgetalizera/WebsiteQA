GIFT INTERNATIONAL FINTECH INSTITUTE The GIFT IFI is set to become a global hub for fintech education, equipping individuals and organizations with the skills needed to thrive in an increasingly digital financial world. Designed to meet the rapidly evolving needs of the fintech sector, the IFI will focus on industry-aligned education, applied research, and innovation. Spearheaded by a consortium led by Ahmedabad University , in collaboration with the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar and the University of California, San Diego , the IFI brings together academic excellence and cutting-edge research. Applied Research & Development As a research hub, the IFI will drive innovation in fintech by collaborating with academic institutions, fintech companies, and government bodies. Its focus on applied R&D will ensure that the fintech ecosystem stays at the forefront of new technologies, products, and market trends, while also contributing to the development of supportive regulatory frameworks. Building a Collaborative Ecosystem The IFIH will foster collaboration among fintech start-ups, academic institutions, government bodies, investors, and accelerators. By creating a shared space for knowledge exchange, the hub will drive innovation, improve market access, and accelerate the development of solutions to key challenges such as financial inclusion and access to credit.
